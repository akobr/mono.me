## What Was Done

### 4. Error-mapping vectors

- **The response model** is the spec's: `status`, `headers`, and one of `json` or `text`. The schema rejects both with `not` and `required`.
- **`schema/errors.schema.json`** has a top-level `key`, which must start with `@`, and vectors of `name`, `read`, `response` and `error` (`status` integer or null, `errorCode` string or null, `transient`).
- **The 27 vectors** are the spec's table:
  - 401 without a body and with a code-less `ErrorResponse`;
  - 403 `AccessDenied` and `MtlsRequired`;
  - 404 without a body, `AnnotationDeleted` and `AnnotationInactive`;
  - 400 `InvalidKey`, 409 and 412;
  - 422 `binding.evaluation`, `binding.limit.steps` and `binding.limit.envelopeDepth`. The limit kinds are the camel-case names of `EvaluationLimitKind`, as `ExceptionExtensions.ToCodeName` writes them;
  - 408, 424, 429 with `Retry-After`, 500, 502 and 504;
  - 501 and 505;
  - 503 without a body, with `KeyEncryptionUnavailable` and `Retry-After`, and with a text body;
  - a blank `ErrorCode`;
  - the effective read for a definitive and a transient error.

  `MtlsRequired` and `AnnotationInactive` reach the provider only as an error code, and the vectors pin that it keeps them.
- **`VectorResponses`** builds a new `HttpResponseMessage` for every request:
  - a `json` body as `StringContent` with `application/json; charset=utf-8`;
  - a `text` body with `text/plain; charset=utf-8`;
  - headers through `TryAddWithoutValidation`, falling back to the content headers.

  It also reads the mode, the keys of an `IConfiguration`, the expected keys, the failure comparison and the mode's path.
- **`ErrorVectorTests`.**
  - `AtStartup`: a required source with `Http.Otherwise` set to the response. `Build()` throws, and the test compares `StatusCode`, `ErrorCode`, `IsTransient` and `Key`, and checks that every request went to the mode's path.
  - `AtRefresh`: a start from a valid document, then the response. `TryRefreshAsync` is false and `Status.LastError` is compared.
  - `TheSuite_HasOneErrorsFile`.
  - **Review fix (`2f6f04db`).** `AtRefresh` compares the complete keys before and after the run, not only `App:Name`, and checks every request of the run.

### 5. Refresh scenarios

- **`schema/refresh.schema.json`** checks the shape of section 5.
  - A step is exactly one of `response` or `timeout`, the latter only `true`.
  - `expect` has `error` exactly when `refreshed` is false, through `oneOf` with `enum` and `not`/`required`.
- **`refresh.json`** was generated by a throwaway script, so the twelve scenarios share one shape: one response, expectation and set of keys per line. The script checked that its formatting kept the JSON identical.
  - Tags have the platform's shape, `W/"r.<22 digits>"`.
  - A 200 body is the full `Configuration` envelope.
- **`RefreshScenarioTests`.**
  - **Setup.** A fresh `EngineFixture`, `STORYTELLER_KEY` from the file, the scenario's mode, and `RefreshInterval = TimeSpan.Zero`.
  - **Timeouts.** A scenario with a timeout step gets `ReadTimeout` of 1 s and an infinite attempt timeout, and its timeout step waits on `Task.Delay(Timeout.Infinite, ct)`.
  - **Checks after each `TryRefreshAsync`:** the result, the keys (`AsEnumerable` without the section entries), the cumulative notifications (`ChangeToken.OnChange` on the reload token), the mode's path and `If-None-Match`, and `LastError` with `ConsecutiveFailures`.
- **Deviations from the spec's table.** Each one makes a scenario prove more:
  - The 401 scenario has a recovering 304 step, so it is "… and the next run recovers".
  - The withheld-secrets-on-a-200 scenario has a plain 200 step that proves the withheld answer stored no tag: "… and store no tag".
  - The invalid document is `{ "App": { "a:b": "x" } }`, with a recovering 304 that still sends tag 1: "… is definitive, and stores no tag".
  - "does not care about" became "does not check".
  - The runner also asserts `ConsecutiveFailures`.
- **Review fix (`2f6f04db`).** The runner required exactly one request per step. That held only because the fixture turns retries off: a scratch run with the provider's default retries made the 503 step send four requests. It now checks the mode's path and the sent tag on every request of the step, so the runner holds with retries, as the vectors say a response answers every request.
- **Mutation check.** Making `408` definitive in `errors.json` and changing the sent tag of a 304 step in `refresh.json` failed exactly `AtStartup` and `AtRefresh` of the 408 vector, and that scenario. The files were then restored.
